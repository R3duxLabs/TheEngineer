# The Engineer Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** July 1, 2025

## Introduction

Welcome to The Engineer! This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and handle your information when you use our desktop application that integrates with the Anthropic Claude API.

## Information We Collect

### Information You Provide

- **Anthropic API Key**: We collect and securely store your Anthropic API key to facilitate communication with the Claude API.
- **Conversation Data**: The content of your conversations with Claude is processed and may be stored locally on your device.
- **User Settings**: Your preferences and configuration settings for the application.
- **Custom Agents**: Any specialized agent configurations you create.

### Information Collected Automatically

- **Application Usage Data**: Information about how you use the application, including features accessed and errors encountered.
- **Performance Data**: Technical information related to the application's performance.

## How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

- Connect to the Anthropic Claude API on your behalf
- Enable conversation history and search functionality
- Provide customized agent experiences
- Improve the application's performance and features
- Troubleshoot issues

## Data Storage and Security

- **Local Storage**: Most of your data is stored locally on your device, including conversations, settings, and custom agents.
- **API Key Storage**: Your Anthropic API key is stored securely on your local device using platform-specific secure storage mechanisms.
- **Data Transmission**: When you communicate with Claude, data is transmitted securely to Anthropic's servers according to their security protocols.

## Data Sharing

We do not share your data with third parties except as follows:

- **Anthropic**: Your conversations and prompts are sent to Anthropic's servers to generate responses using the Claude API.
- **Legal Requirements**: We may disclose your information if required to do so by law or in response to valid legal requests.

## Your Rights and Choices

You have the right to:

- **Access**: View the data stored locally by the application.
- **Delete**: Remove your conversation history and custom agents from the application.
- **Control**: Manage your settings, including whether to save conversations.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy within the application or on our website.

## Third-Party Services
